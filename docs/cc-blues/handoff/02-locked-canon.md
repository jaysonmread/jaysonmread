## 1. The six verbs (LOCKED 2026-07-10)

> **RAIL assembles · RIVER carries · ROAD moves · RECORD spins · READING tells · RADIO transmits**

Rationale for the two that were contested (so they don't reopen):
- **RECORD spins** — "fixes" (ChatGPT's original) was rejected: "fix" reads as *repair* and
  collides with the Repair Track; a verb that needs a footnote fails the democratic register.
  "keeps" was also rejected because *keeping* is what Record and Reading share (both are
  evidence worlds), so it can't name either one. "spins" names Record's distinctive power
  (only Record can make the music sound again) in the music's own vernacular (DJs/jukeboxes spin).
- **READING tells** — "interprets" was rejected as over-claiming: much of Reading merely
  states facts. Reading has **three registers** — **ledger** (facts: discographies, session
  logs, catalogs), **witness** (firsthand: memoirs, interviews, oral history), **argument**
  (interpretation: criticism, scholarship). "tells" is the honest umbrella over all three.

## 2. The Record world charter & the performance principle

- **Record = "sound made into things."** Labels, discs, vinyl — and the pre-phonograph
  objects (sheet music, piano rolls; the 1912 published blues preceded the 1920 first blues
  disc) and non-commercial objects (field discs, test pressings, unissued takes).
- **Internal chain:** performance → take/matrix → issued object → reissue.
- **Performances are EVENTS, never Record objects — even when a recording exists** (Mr.
  Read's ruling; anti-survivorship-bias). Most blues performances left no object; if
  performances lived in Record, the architecture would silently claim only recorded music
  happened. Events link to artifacts via `survives_as`; objects link back via `documents`;
  they never merge.
- **Sheet music sits on the fork:** `worlds: [record, reading]` — a Record object by
  commerce, Reading material by nature. (Boundary test: can it make the music sound again
  without someone reading words? Disc = yes; piano roll = yes; sheet music = no.)
- **Record + Reading are the two evidence worlds** (grooves / ink). The other four are
  interpretive lenses. Record is where makers testify directly; Reading is where (for long
  stretches) others did the talking. Circle cars carry person-level economics (e.g. Lead
  Belly suing John Lomax over withheld earnings); Record carries system-level economics
  (the industry that made sound ownable).

## 3. The no-AI citation law (STRUCTURAL, Mr. Read's hardest line — triple-confirmed)

Confirmed independently by Mr. Read, the Framing Standard, AND ChatGPT.

- **Public citations may come ONLY from real sources**, `kind ∈ {scholarly, primary,
  periodical, liner, archive, discography, reference}`.
- **Deep-research reports (Gemini DR, etc.) and Wikipedia harvests live in a private `_leads`
  field.** Their only job is to point at the real footnote; then the real source is read and
  cited, and the lead is discarded from the record. `_leads` is never rendered and forbidden
  in `sources[]` by the linter.
- **The word "AI" never appears in public output.** Mentioning AI would, in Mr. Read's
  words, "completely discredit my entire process." This is existential to the project.
- **In the image pipeline:** generated art supplies only the *frame and empty windows*.
  Generated lettering is placeholder texture, never facts — every title/date/number/citation
  is overlaid deterministically from the canonical DB. **No generated historical likenesses**
  — real archival photographs composited into standardized empty portrait windows only.
- **`[DR-GAP]` (internal marker) blocks publication.** Publicly, a gap renders as an honest
  historical condition — "Source gap / Date disputed / Not yet corroborated" — never as
  internal pipeline language. (ChatGPT correctly flagged that a literal public "DR-GAP" badge
  leaks internal workflow terms.)

## 4. The Framing Standard (governs ALL public language & organization)

Source: `CC Blues Vault/_canon/2026-06-17_FRAMING_STANDARD_BLACK_AMERICAN_MUSIC.md` (LOCKED).
Two laws: **(1) Honor the music on its own terms** (groove, improvisation, call-and-response,
Olly Wilson's heterogeneous sound ideal — deliberate craft, not primitive root or sociological
symptom). **(2) Name the extraction** (authorship, credit, ownership, money — "influence" often
launders racial capitalism).

Register: **democratic + encyclopedic** (welcomes the reader, never guards the door; the maker
is a full author and intellectual). Voice = **"Pete"** (the scalable card rule: *name the maker,
the music, the musical action, the evidence, the money/mediation when it matters*).

**Retire → Use (with why):**
| Retire | Use |
|---|---|
| discovered / rediscovered | recorded · documented · located · made contact with · contracted |
| raw / primitive / primal | timbrally complex · cultivated · engineered · conceptually rigorous |
| authentic | traditional · regional · non-commercial (or historicize directly) |
| natural / instinctual rhythm | masterful · polyrhythmic · cultivated technique |
| **substrate / stratum / foundation-for / pre-genre** (**"substrate" is BANNED**) | co-existing tradition · neighboring practice · "before blues circulated as a commercial category" |
| influence / transmission (when commercial) | the exact verb: borrowed · adapted · reworked · credited / miscredited / marketed / profited from / extracted |
| genius (as unlearned savant) | innovator · radical intellectual · master practitioner · architect |
| "race records," period slurs | quote-and-date: keep in quotes, attribute, contextualize — never the archive's own voice |
| slaves / the poor | enslaved people · poor people |

**"Spine" is dead** (a book metaphor in a train world, and an evolutionary-ladder frame the
standard retires). Importance is the **API score** (§Locked-canon 6 / see `01`), stated as
*landmark / master practitioner*, shown-then-named.

**Three refused habits:** evolutionary language (no ladder); collector-centered revelation (a
musician is not "made real" when a white collector finds him — those events are access /
documentation / business / mediation, the subject stays the artist); vibe-as-evidence
(earthy/untouched/instinctive = racist work even as praise).

**The yard's primary navigation** is four functional-aesthetic nodes, NOT a regional grid:
(1) The Sacred & the Testimonial · (2) The Dance & the Groove · (3) The Work & the Commentary
· (4) The Theatrical & the Cosmopolitan. Region/era/scene/lineage are secondary facets.

**The Circle** = the *full* circle (producers, label founders, folklorists, family,
community), where authorship/credit/economics get surfaced — without making mediators the
protagonists.

## 5. The Koonaklaster (Fahey's law — the archive's epistemology)

From John Fahey's essay "The Nature of Reality" (captured locally in Zone 03). Adopted as
standing guardrail:
- **R11 — no each-to-all edges, ever.** Only enumerated, pairwise, **cited** edges. A world
  may be *computed from* edges; it may never be *entered as a premise*. (Dense clusters that
  emerge honestly are real; asserted worlds are refused at the gate.)
- **R12 — corpus-boundary.** Every aggregate claim names its sample, region, era. ("The
  Anthology was southeastern-coast weighted while wearing the nation's name… No Readville.")
  The answer to narrowness is *widening the sample*, never renaming the nation.
- **R13 — circularity caveat.** Describing a people from their recorded performances is
  probably circular, so every synthesis is *a* reading, not *THE* reading, and says so —
  **visible masks, never a hidden narrator.**
- **Greil Marcus is the structural foil** (asserted worlds vs. enumerated edges); the
  Marcus→Hutchison projection is kept as a REJECTED exhibit.

## 6. Promotion gate (private → public)

`status: draft → review → published`. **Auto-draft, Mr. Read releases.** Verified entries
auto-stage as public-but-unpublished drafts; he flips them live in batches. Keeps his
editorial veto without mechanical work. A `[DR-GAP]` or unmet-source condition blocks
`published: true`.

## 7. UI contract (from the reconciled ChatGPT interface spec)

- **Every clickable = a schema field wearing a costume**, but **clicks write only to
  ephemeral `ui_state`** (viewport, pinned thread, open station, time cursor, etc.) — never to
  the canonical entry. Editorial writes happen only in the governed authoring system.
- **Diegetic where the metaphor clarifies the operation; documentary (plain) where evidence
  and accessibility require it.** Don't turn every field into a control or every action into
  railroad theater.
- **Production hierarchy (in priority order):** canonical field → historical action/evidence
  → accessible control → diegetic costume → motion/ornament.
- **READ MODE is the accessibility guarantee** and the authoritative linear rendering — not a
  lesser site.

## 8. Pete-voice disclosure

The narrating voice ("Pete") is a curated register modeled on Pete Seeger — who is himself a
*source* in the archive (camp 13). Per R13 (visible masks), the public **self-dossier** must
disclose that the narrator is a positioned mask, with eyes open to the folk revival's
failures (outsider romance, authenticity-policing, the extractive middleman).
